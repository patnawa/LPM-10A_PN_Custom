Changes from PN 2.6:
- FLASH retries back off from 4 to 8 to 16 seconds; keep the working window
  for that session. Full minimum 1500 ms hold / 1000 ms PHY-off periods.
- Length: '~' means a partial average (1-3 of 4 runs yielded a reading).
  Rerun and check the connection. '=' does not certify accuracy/stability.
- OVR replaces a number that cannot fit the display representation, instead
  of wrapping into a tiny false reading. Other units may still fit.
- Queued NVP/Zero redraws cannot paint another screen.
- Battery debounce is explicitly cleared at boot, preserving three-sample arming.
