Includes PN2.27's carrier GPIO optimization and changes Analog modulation:
- Mean Analog frequency is nominally 816.832 Hz, close to RX PN1.24's nominal filter bin.
- The Analog button reads Analog 817 Hz in both language settings.
- The shared timer stays at 101 us; Digital timing and carrier are retained.
- A phase accumulator produces 50% envelope duty over its repeating period.
  Individual half-cycles use six or seven timer ticks
  (606 / 707 us). Better mean alignment does not mean less edge jitter.

The modeled receiver results support the alignment change. They do not prove
better physical gain, range, localization or continuous audio performance.

Before promotion, 516 TX SDK tests passed. Both images were exercised across
60,006 actual-code timer IRQs and 2,096 modeled RX acquisitions. PN2.27A
passed 17,834 key/navigation interrupt placements. At the weak Analog test
level, it detected 127/127 modeled phases versus PN2.27's 124/127 on both
RX PN1.24 and PN1.23G. This is an ideal input model, not a physical range test.
