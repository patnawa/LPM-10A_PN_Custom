Purpose: investigate the owner's approximately one-second continuous Digital
sound after moving away or pressing TX Pause, also reported for RX key feedback.
The physical cause is not yet established. PN 1.13 is a test candidate, not a

Later video evidence shows a train of roughly 50 ms pulses continuing for
0.8-1.0 seconds after TX Digital-to-Analog selection changes. The clip does
not establish the earlier Pause/RX-key test. Installed-image identity remains
unverified. See docs/TONE-CLIP-DIAGNOSIS-2026-09-20.md for measurements and the
byte-identical PN1.13 copy using the stock basename, also reported unchanged. That copy is
still PN1.13, not stock firmware; RX filename acceptance is not established.

The shared BEEP and quiet-gap countdowns now run from TIM5, the timer already
driving the speaker. TIM1 no longer decrements these two counters. Delayed TIM1
delivery therefore cannot stretch an active pulse while TIM5 keeps running.
One audio tick is nominally 1.000625 ms; normal tones are approximately 30 ms
and uncertainty/key-confirmation tones approximately 100 ms.

Digital detection, Analog detection, ADC sampling, sensitivity and strength
mapping retain PN 1.12 behavior. This audio change affects all receiver modes
and confirmation tones. RECENT, battery monitoring, key scanning and auto-off
remain on TIM1. No new RAM or gain/drive adjustment is introduced.

The earlier proposed device check covered Digital pause/move-away, RX key
feedback, Analog release and ordinary sweeping. Further RX update trials are
deferred until image compatibility and update acceptance can be verified.
PN 1.12 remains archived for comparison, not as a proven rollback image.

See docs/TONE-AUDIO-CLOCK-PN1.13-PN2.14-2026-09-20.md for software verification
and limits. Software timing uses modeled timer delivery, not measured hardware
latency or physical comparison with Fluke.
