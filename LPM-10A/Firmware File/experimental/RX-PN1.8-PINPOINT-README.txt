Hardware follow-up, 2026-09-19: the owner reports "Tested pass" for PN 1.8.
Recorded as a functional device test pass. No detailed test matrix or
quantitative range/cable-selection measurements accompanied the report.

Use TX Digital with the existing RX digital mode. Buttons work as before.

Changes from PN 1.7:
- Replace five broad strength grades with an interpolated quiet interval.
- Spread feedback over a wider range so strong signals do not all receive
  the fastest rate as early as in PN 1.7.
- Keep pulses at 30 ms; stronger accepted signals shorten the quiet interval
  from 160 toward 20 ms. Existing pulses and quiet intervals finish normally.
- Hold proposed gap changes below 3 ms to reduce jitter. Compare against the
  last published interval so slow strength changes can accumulate and update.

The strength curve uses score/gap knots:
  0/160, 800/130, 2400/105, 7200/75, 24000/45, 88000/20.
Gaps are in milliseconds. Scores are uncalibrated ADC contrast, not distance.
Active feedback can retain a gap up to 2 ms from its raw target, including
at the curve endpoints. No quantified listening comparison was supplied.

Includes all PN 1.7 fixes. Detection eligibility, the trimmed strength metric,
sample timing, first-rejected-window release, 300 ms audio freshness, 800 ms
power keepalive, key tones, analog/mains operation and device binding are
retained. Three existing blocks change; no extra persistent RAM or image growth.
The published and owner-tested PN 1.7 binary is preserved.

Actual firmware emulation distinguishes synthetic signals that PN 1.7 sounded
identically. This establishes finer feedback, not improved physical range or
guaranteed selection of the correct cable. 104 test groups passed: 81 existing
image/profile and RX groups plus 23 PN 1.8 groups. The saved binary matches its
deterministic rebuild and checksum. The separate owner-reported device pass is
recorded above. Published without binary changes:
  https://github.com/patnawa/LPM-10A_PN_Custom/releases/tag/rx-v1.8
