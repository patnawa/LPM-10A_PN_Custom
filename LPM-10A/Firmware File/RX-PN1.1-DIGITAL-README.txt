Includes the existing PN 1.0 battery-recovery fix and a replacement digital
detector in the original function's 336-byte footprint:
- Retain the stock path accepting two exact sliding B6B6 matches.
- Add correlation across eight bit rotations of all 48 samples, allowing
  at most four bad bits overall and two per 16-sample block.
- Require contrast: absolute deviations from the trimmed mean must total
  at least 192 ADC counts. Retain the existing high-sample sum and PA2 gates.
  The contrast floor needs bench calibration; very weak signals can fail it.

This does NOT implement oversampling, sub-slot phase tracking, gain changes
or a strength display. ADC pipeline, timers, analogue and mains modes, beep
cadence and 800-ms signal hold remain unchanged. No persistent RAM or image
growth. UID binding and vendor version-page handling are untouched, so the
internal vendor version string remains 3.0.0; identify this build by its hash.
