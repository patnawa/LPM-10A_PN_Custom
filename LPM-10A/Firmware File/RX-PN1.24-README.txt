RX PN1.24 - gain recovery, sample freshness and Analog efficiency
2026-09-22 - owner-tested release

Based on the exact PN1.23G image whose Digital gain-change audio fix was
confirmed by the owner. TX PN2.26 remains compatible.

Owner confirmation: "1.24 Tested pass no signal drop on digital and analog."
This is a device result for signal continuity. Numerical performance and
noise results in the analysis report are CPU-model measurements.

Use APP_LPM-10RX_PN1.24-gain-precision-update.bin with the RX bootloader.
Do not copy the raw APP_LPM-10RX_PN1.24-gain-precision.bin as an update.
After updating, verify the RX identity is PN1.24.TXT.
RX-PN1.24-SHA256SUMS.txt lists both artifact hashes.

Changes:
- Automatic gain corrections can occur every 1 second instead of 2.5 seconds.
  The knob ceiling and existing hysteresis thresholds are preserved.
- Automatic decisions require a complete acquisition at the current gain.
- Expired sample windows cannot renew audio feedback after a foreground stall.
  Digital's bounded audio continuity across gain changes remains included.
- Analog checks its target before evaluating the noise bins. Quiet input
  needs about 97% fewer analyzer instructions in the CPU model, with unchanged
  detection decisions and strength mapping in the differential test sweep.

Build and CPU tests do not establish physical pickup distance or calibrated
precision. The owner confirmed both modes work without signal drops. Hardware
gain settling, noise margins and receiver-to-receiver variation have not been
quantitatively characterized by that confirmation.
