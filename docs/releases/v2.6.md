# TX PN 2.6 — SCAN timing, reliable resume and battery recovery

Unofficial firmware for the **LPM-10A TX tester**, based on official V2.0.7.
The owner reported testing the new TX and RX firmware on 2026-09-19 and that
"everything work perfect". This is an owner-reported functional pass on their
units, not a certification of all hardware revisions or operating conditions.

## Changes since PN 2.4

- Fix the digital SCAN wrap: the first high slot now gets all 50 timer ticks.
- Bypass the two SCAN debug-logging blocks reached from TIM2, removing their
  formatting, critical sections and task-context queue sends from that path.
- Fix SCAN resume: invalidate the cached carrier level before enabling, so
  the first resumed tick drives the requested level.
- Clear battery debounce when voltage recovery or charging cancels shutdown;
  the next low-voltage episode must accumulate three fresh low samples.
- Harden SDK patch prerequisites, relocated subset builds, allocation sizing
  and image/range validation. Includes the earlier unpublished PN 2.5 work.

Carrier configuration, digital B6B6 code, timer programming, 825 Hz waveform,
watchdog/backlight cadence and existing UI behavior are retained. Previous
firmware binaries remain available. This release does not alter the RX image.

## Verification and limits

The full TX verifier passes, including 61 screen states in both languages,
39 SCAN checks, the 12 audit regression tests and assembler self-tests.
The release binary is byte-identical to the owner-tested candidate.

The [FLASH/length audit](https://github.com/patnawa/LPM-10A_PN_Custom/blob/v2.6/docs/FLASH-LENGTH-AUDIT-2026-09-19.md)
documents open edge cases: slow-link recovery can starve acquisition, averaging
can retain low-confidence results, pair voting can hide differences, and stale
redraw/malformed-input cases remain. Those paths were not changed in PN 2.6.
The general hardware report is not a detailed PoE-supply or edge-case retest.

## TX update and rollback

1. Check the SHA-256: `certutil -hashfile LPM-10A-TX_PN2.6.bin SHA256`.
2. Power off the tester; hold **M + Power** until the update screen appears.
3. Connect USB-C and copy the TX binary to the removable drive. Do not unplug
   during the update.
4. Long-press Power off, then power on. About should read `Software:PN 2.6`.

If the file is refused, the documented stock filename is
`LPM-10A-TX_V2.0.7_260610.bin`. Rollback uses the same TX procedure with the
official V2.0.7 image obtained from FNIRSI. Retain your stock recovery file;
the vendor's original image is not attached. Flash at your own risk.
